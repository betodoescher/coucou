## ✨ What's new

Mochi just moved from "agent watcher" to **your whole day, at the top of your screen**. Cursor and Kiro join Claude Code, your plans' usage is one glance away, and your tasks, habits and notes now live right in the island.

### 🤖 Every agent, one island
- **Cursor and Kiro sessions, live.** Follow their turns step by step and **approve their shell commands** with Allow / Deny, just like Claude Code. Hooks install with a backup and a diff, never blind.
- **Claude Code gets its own session pill**, like Cursor and Kiro: it appears while a session runs and leaves when it ends.

### 💬 Chat on the plans you already pay for
- **Chat through your Cursor plan** (Cursor CLI) or **your Kiro plan** (Kiro CLI), next to the Claude API. Read-only: the agent answers, it can't touch your files.
- **Automatic fallback.** Out of credits on one plan? The chat hands over to the next one (Cursor → Kiro → API).

### 🏠 A home card that actually helps
- **Weather** for your city (Open-Meteo, no key, no account): now, high and low, chance of rain. Type it however you like, e.g. "Florianópolis SC" or "São Paulo, Brasil".
- **Your next task, today's habits, and how many agents are running or waiting**, all in one card.
- **AI usage, as `/usage` shows it:** `Cursor 70% · Kiro 1.9% · Claude 1.2M`. Hover for the details: plan, Auto/API split, credits left, reset date.

### ✅ Today: tasks, notes and habits
- **Tasks** with a day, priority, list and an optional **reminder time**. The island opens when it's due, and nudges you at 9:00 and 14:00 on and before the due day.
- **Quick notes** right beside your tasks.
- **Daily habits with streaks** 🔥.

### 🐙 GitHub on watch
- **Open PRs across your orgs and failed Actions.** The island opens on CI failures and review requests.

### 🪟 Island, your way
- **Drag the island anywhere.** It remembers where you left it, and the tray puts it back at the top.
- **Shrink the open island** with one click, even when pinned.
- **Auto-hide toggle:** keep the compact island on screen instead of letting it hide after a minute.
- **Better on GNOME** (Linux): the island sits at the top of the screen and survives Super+D.

### 🔒 Still private by design
No telemetry, no account. Keys stay in your system keychain, usage is read from your agents' own CLIs and logs, and the app only talks to the services you turn on.
